SECTION 01 Executive Summary

What We Are Building & Why It Wins

🏗️ Phase 1 — Foundation

Sell, buy, manage stock, and set up your team from day one. A working product that handles daily operations natively, including ZATCA Phase 1 QR codes and full receipt/payment workflows.

📈 Phase 2 — Full Accounting & Scale

Unlock the double-entry accounting engine, payroll & GOSI, POS, multi-branch, and the VAT module. Everything a growing Saudi SME needs to scale operations confidently.

📊 Phase 3 — Charts, Reports & ZATCA

Full ZATCA Phase 2 compliance with UBL 2.1 and real-time portal submission. Complete reporting suite for every module, plus interactive dashboards and deep financial analytics.

🚀 Phase 4 - Mobile & AI

A Flutter companion mobile app (modeled on Qoyod's full feature set) with Firebase push notifications, plus an advanced AI layer including Arabic accounting chat, WhatsApp bot, and receipt OCR.
